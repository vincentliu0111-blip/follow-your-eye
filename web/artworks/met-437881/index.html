LOOK CLOSELY

Vermeer’s Young Woman with a Water Pitcher: following the window light

The observations and questions below are Follow Your Eye editorial, based on the image; they are not museum commentary.

Start at the window

The window occupies the left edge rather than the middle. Follow its vertical frame toward the woman’s extended arm, then look at the light on her white covering. Which path do you take through the room when you begin at its edge?

Compare quiet and busy areas

The broad wall behind the figure is relatively quiet. In front, the red patterned textile contains many smaller shapes. Move your attention between these areas. Does the detailed foreground hold you, or send you back toward the face and white linen?

Notice what light separates

Look at the pale cloth beside the darker blue clothing, then at the pitcher against its surroundings. You do not have to identify every object to follow those contrasts. Ask which small change of brightness makes the scene easier to read for you.

How can I look at the light in this Vermeer?

Try following a path from the left-hand window to the white linen and then to the pitcher. Compare those light areas with the darker clothing and patterned foreground.
